Our Story

A Decade of Helping Australians with Technology

XTS Tech Support was founded in 2016 by a group of passionate IT professionals who were frustrated by the overpriced, jargon-heavy tech support industry. We believed that everyday Australians deserved honest, affordable and friendly help with their technology - without being made to feel stupid for asking.

Starting with just three technicians in Sydney, we quickly grew as word spread that XTS was different. We showed up on time, explained things in plain language, and only charged when the job was done. By 2020 we had expanded to remote support services, allowing us to help customers anywhere in Australia within minutes.

Today, XTS Tech Support has over 200 trained technicians operating in every major city and regional centre across Australia. We have helped more than 15,000 customers - from pensioners getting their first tablet set up to small businesses setting up entire office networks. Our mission has not changed: to be the tech friends you wish you had.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These values guide every decision we make and every interaction we have with our customers.

Honesty First

We tell it straight. No upselling services you do not need. No inflating problems to charge more. If your issue has a simple free fix, we will tell you.

People-Centred

We treat every customer with patience and respect. We never make you feel silly for not knowing something. Everyone starts somewhere.

Reliability

We show up on time. We do what we say we will do. You can count on XTS to be there when you need us - 7 days a week.

Continuous Learning

Technology changes fast. Our technicians undergo regular training to stay current with the latest hardware, software and security practices.

Fair Value

Premium service does not have to mean premium prices. We keep our rates competitive and always transparent so you know exactly what you are paying for.

Sustainability

We encourage repair over replacement where possible, reducing electronic waste. When devices cannot be repaired, we guide customers to responsible recycling options.
